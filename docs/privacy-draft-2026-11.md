# Privacy policy — draft for review (November 2026)

**Not legal advice — check with a lawyer before publishing.** Drafted for Eastwood Community Football Club CIC from what the website and app actually do as of version 3.104.0. Nothing here is live: the current page at eastwoodfc.com/privacy-policy is unchanged.

**Three things in the live policy are no longer true and must change when this goes up:**
1. It says "We do not run advertising trackers." The site carries two Meta (Facebook) pixels and, when set, Google Analytics, and its own cookie banner says cookies are used "to show the club's posts to people who have visited".
2. It says "We do not build a profile of you." The activity log now records what each supporter and staff member does, to understand behaviour patterns, sales and efficiency.
3. It lists Football Web Pages under "where it is held". They supply fixtures and results only and never receive anyone's personal data, so they come off that list.

## Privacy policy

This page explains what Eastwood Football Club does with information about you, on eastwoodfc.com and in the Team EastwoodFC app. It is written for this club, not copied from a template.

**Who we are.** Eastwood Community Football Club CIC (company number 09196902), Coronation Park, Chewton Street, Eastwood, Nottinghamshire NG16 3HB, is the data controller. Eastwood FC Ventures Ltd (company number 17358254), at the same address, runs the club's commercial side (shop, tickets sold through Stripe, its own bar till) and is the controller for sales it makes. Contact for anything on this page: info@eastwoodfc.com.

### Your MyEastwood account

You can read the website and use most of the app without an account. To see your tickets, vote, post, or claim a free ticket you make a MyEastwood account. We keep:

- your email address and a password (stored by Supabase in a form we cannot read), or your Apple or Google sign-in if you choose one. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we get a relay address from Apple and link it to the email you booked with;
- your name, if you give it, and your mobile number if you add it (checked with a code sent by text);
- your MyEastwood ID number (for example EFC-101234), which stays with your account for good. Long-standing supporters can ask for an old season ticket number.

The same account works on the website and in the app.

### Tickets, claims and bookings

When you buy or claim a ticket, a hospitality place, a shirt, a food token or a place at an event, or sign up for the half-time show, the academy or a trial, we keep your name, email address, what you booked, how many people it is for and, for free school tickets, which school the ticket came from. We record when your ticket is scanned at the turnstile, including scans that are turned away (for example a ticket already used). Your booking is linked to your MyEastwood account by your email address.

### Payments

We never see or store your full card number.

- **Online:** card payments are taken by **Stripe** (for Eastwood FC Ventures Ltd). Stripe tells us when a payment succeeds, fails, is refunded or disputed.
- **At the ground:** the bars and stands use **Square** tills, one account for Eastwood Community Football Club CIC and one for Eastwood FC Ventures Ltd. For each sale, refund or cancelled sale we receive the amount, the time, which till and which member of staff served, the card type and its last four digits, and your email address if you gave Square one for a receipt.

### What we record about how the site and app are used (the activity log)

We keep a log of actions taken on our systems by everyone — supporters, guests and club staff, including the chairman — so we can run match days well, spot problems and fraud, understand what people come for and buy, and see how efficiently we work. Each entry holds:

- who did it, where known: name, email address, MyEastwood ID or staff login;
- what was done: for example signing in or failing to sign in, requesting a password reset, claiming or buying a ticket, a ticket scan and its result, a payment, refund or dispute, a till sale, a job application, and, for staff, every change made in the club's systems and every supporter record viewed or exported;
- when, and from where: the time, whether it was the website, the iPhone app, the Android app, a till or staff admin, the IP address and the device or browser type.

Passwords are never recorded. The log cannot be edited or deleted by anyone at the club. Staff see only the part their role needs: match-day staff see scans only, and reporting staff see totals with no names.

We use the log to look at patterns, for example how many people who claim a free ticket come back and pay, or how long the bar queue takes at half-time. This is analysis by the club for running the club; it is not used to make automatic decisions about you and is not sold or given to anyone else.

### Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website uses cookies to measure how it is used (Google Analytics) and two Meta (Facebook) pixels, which tell Meta when someone visits, buys or signs up, so we can see whether our posts work and show the club's posts to people who have visited. You can refuse these cookies in the banner; the site works without them. The app does not contain advertising trackers.

### The Team EastwoodFC app

- **Notifications:** if you turn on match alerts, your phone's push token is stored so we can send them, through **Expo**. You choose which alerts you get and can turn them off at any time.
- **Votes and posts:** your Man of the Match votes and anything you post in the match centre are kept with your account. Supporters' posts are read by a club editor before they appear.
- **Club staff and Eastwood Flex runners:** staff and approved runners can use a driver mode to deliver tickets and flyers. Only while they are on a run with the app open, and only after they allow it, the app uses their phone's precise location for turn-by-turn directions (from **Google Maps**) and so the club can see where a run has got to. When a drop is marked done we keep the time, the place and any photo taken. Supporters' locations are never collected.

### Job applications ("Join the team")

If you apply to work or volunteer with the club, including as an Eastwood Flex runner, we keep your name, email address, mobile number, the role you applied for, whether you want paid or volunteer work, and the video you send. Videos are stored privately and only the people deciding on applications can watch them.

### Emails and our CRM

Booking confirmations, tickets and club emails are sent through **GoHighLevel (LeadConnector)**, which also holds our supporter contact list. We email you about what you booked. We only send news and offers if you ticked to hear from us, and every one has a way to unsubscribe.

### If you contact us

When you email or call us, we keep what you send for as long as we need it to deal with it and to keep a record of club business.

### Who holds it for us

These companies process information on our instructions only:

| Who | What for |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase (EU) | MyEastwood accounts, the activity log, app data, job videos |
| Stripe | Online card payments |
| Square | Bar and stand tills (CIC and Ventures) |
| GoHighLevel / LeadConnector | Emails and the supporter contact list |
| Twilio | Text messages with sign-in and verification codes |
| Expo | App push notifications |
| Google | Google Maps directions for runs; Google sign-in if you choose it; Google Analytics on the website |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple if you choose it; app distribution |
| Meta | Website pixels (see Cookies) |
| Our web host and Cloudflare | Running and protecting the website, including standard server logs |
| YouTube | Videos embedded on the site, only once you press play |

Fixtures, results and tables come from Football Web Pages; your browser does not contact them and they receive no information about you.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK. Where your information goes outside the UK, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires (such as the UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement).

### How long we keep things

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Activity log | 6 years from the action |
| Payment and booking records | 6 years from the end of the financial year they belong to (the period HMRC requires for accounts) |
| Your MyEastwood account | Until you delete it. Anything tied to a payment is then kept for the 6 years above; everything else goes within 30 days |
| Votes and posts | Until you delete them or your account |
| Job applications and videos | 12 months from the application, unless you are taken on, when they move to your staff record |
| Marketing contact list | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you |
| Push tokens | Until you turn alerts off, sign out or delete your account |
| Run locations and delivery photos | 6 years, as a record of what was delivered where |

### What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not let other companies use it for their own purposes.

### Deleting your account

In the app, go to your account and tap Delete account. Or email info@eastwoodfc.com from the address you signed up with and we will do it within a month. Your account, votes and posts are deleted; records the law requires us to keep (payments, and the activity log entries linked to them) are kept for the periods above and then deleted.

### Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it, object to us using it for marketing or analysis, and ask for it in a form you can take elsewhere. Write to info@eastwoodfc.com and we will respond within a month. If you are not happy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

### Why we are allowed to use it

- To give you what you booked or bought, and run your account: because it is needed for that contract.
- Payment and accounting records: because the law requires it.
- The activity log, fraud checks, security and understanding how the club runs: the club's legitimate interests in running match days safely and the club sustainably. You can object (see Your rights).
- Marketing emails, analytics and Meta pixels: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- Job applications: to consider you for the role you applied for.
